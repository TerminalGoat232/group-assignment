Acknowledgement of Country & First Nations Commitment

Reconciliation & Inclusion

Group G03 and Susnium™ Clean Energy respectfully acknowledge the Wurundjeri Woi-wurrung people of the Kulin Nation, the Traditional Owners and Custodians of the lands on which Swinburne University of Technology’s Melbourne campuses are located, as well as Traditional Custodians across all Country where clean energy infrastructure is researched and deployed. We pay our deepest respects to Elders past and present, and recognise that sovereignty was never ceded.

Why This Matters to Susnium

Context & Ecological Stewardship: Solar arrays, wind turbines, and hydro storage networks depend directly on land, water, and sky that Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples have cared for sustainably for over 65,000 years. For Group G03, engineering a clean energy future means respecting Cultural Heritage, upholding Free, Prior and Informed Consent (FPIC), and co-designing regional microgrids in genuine partnership with Traditional Owner communities.

Inclusive Career Pathways

Supporting First Nations Talent: We actively encourage applications from Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander engineers, researchers, and legal professionals across all Susnium technical and governance roles, including our Energy Researcher (ENRSC) and Legal Counsel (LGCSL) openings, supported by culturally safe mentorship and flexible application pathways via our Application Portal.
