ENERGY TRADER (ENTRD)

Buys and sells electricity and related energy products to maximize the value of the company's renewable generation while managing market risk.

View details
The job is included with health insurance, paid time off, retirement plan and multiple oppoturnities for growing and developing career.

Salary:

  • $90,000 / year

Reporting line:

  • Chief Operating Officer (COO)

Responsibilities:

  • Trade power on day-ahead, intraday and forward markets to sell the company's renewable output at the best available price.
  • Forecast generation, demand and prices, and adjust trading positions as weather and market conditions change.
  • Monitor and manage market, credit and volume risk within approved trading limits.
  • Trade renewable energy certificates, guarantees of origin and carbon credits where applicable.
  • Work with asset managers, operations and project teams on plant availability and balancing needs.
  • Report on trading performance, positions and market trends to management.

Requirements:

  1. Bachelor's degree in finance, economics, engineering, mathematics or a related field.
  2. 2-4 years of experience in energy trading, power markets or commodity trading.
  3. Solid understanding of electricity markets, balancing mechanisms and price drivers.
  4. Strong analytical and quantitative skills, with proficiency in Excel and Python or similar tools.
  5. Ability to make fast decisions under pressure and communicate clearly with technical and commercial teams.
  6. Proficient in English, both written and spoken.
